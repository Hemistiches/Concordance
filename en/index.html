The name

In music, concordance is the agreement of voices that sound true together.

For a medical device, it is the agreement between what you place on the market and what the Regulation requires: the qualification of the product, its class, the certificate that covers it, the deadlines that apply to it, and the obligations of each operator in the chain.

The test does not tell you whether your product complies. It identifies where concordance is not established, and what it takes to establish it.

Where does your concordance stand?

Seven to twelve questions, depending on your role and your product. The path adapts: manufacturer established in or outside the Union, distributor or importer, medical device or in vitro diagnostic device.

At the end, you can choose to answer a few questions about your insurance. This section checks whether you have taken the steps needed to have sufficient financial coverage for your liability, as the Regulation requires of manufacturers.

What is at stake now

Medical device compliance is won in the technical file, but also in the texts: qualification, status, transitional regimes, allocation of roles. The test reviews these six points, according to your role.

  • Legacy certificatesThe transition ends on 31 December 2027 for class III and class IIb implantable devices, with exceptions, and on 31 December 2028 for other devices. Subject to conditions.
  • In vitro diagnostics31 December 2027 for class D, 2028 for class C, 2029 for class B and class A sterile. For the last two, an application must be lodged by 26 May 2027 at the latest.
  • EUDAMEDFour modules mandatory since 28 May 2026. Devices already on the market must be registered by the end of November 2026.
  • Qualification and classWhat your product legally is, software included, drives the conformity route, the budget and the timetable.
  • Operators' rolesManufacturer, authorised representative, importer, distributor: each has its own obligations, and a distributor can become a manufacturer without knowing it.
  • The reform under wayThe simplification proposal of 16 December 2025 has not been adopted. Until it is, the current rules and deadlines apply.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to start with the orientation consultation?

No. It is useful if you do not know where to start; it is never required. If you know what you need, ask for that assignment directly. Where the consultation prepares an assignment engaged within thirty days, its €350 excl. VAT is deducted from the package, once, and the work done is carried over.

How do I know whether my matter falls under the starting price or a quote?

Each service describes the simple situation its starting price applies to. Software, artificial intelligence, a product range, a borderline product, a complex transition, combining the distributor and importer roles or several entities take you beyond it. The test already tells you. The quote is agreed before any work begins, and nothing extra is invoiced without your agreement.

Is CE marking guaranteed?

No. Neither CE marking nor a decision of an authority or a notified body can be guaranteed. The commitment covers the means, the method, the scope and the timing. I do not build the technical documentation or run the certification: I secure its legal framework.

Can you act as our authorised representative in the Union?

No. I do not act as authorised representative. I help you define what you expect from one, compare the offers you receive and review the mandate, without receiving any remuneration from a provider.

How are subscription hours counted?

All time specific to your file uses the month's volume: questions, preparation, specific research, meetings, personalised watch. You receive a statement of hours with each invoice. Unused hours are not carried over. Beyond the volume, an overrun only takes place with your agreement, at €180 excl. VAT per hour. There is no emergency on-call service.

Do you work on contracts governed by foreign law?

Yes, with specific scoping. Depending on the question, I provide guidance, to be confirmed by counsel qualified in the law concerned, or I arrange their involvement; their fees are separate and agreed in advance.

Does the reform of the Regulations change anything today?

Not yet. The simplification proposal of 16 December 2025 has not been adopted. Until it is, the current rules and deadlines apply, and they are the ones the test and the report use.
